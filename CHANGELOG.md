# What's New in Embara

A plain-language history of changes. For the latest version's store text, see the
"What's new" section on Google Play.

## 1.4.0
- **Location works.** TREK's "Use my current location" button and the new mobile quick-capture
  sheet can now ask for your position. Embara asks you first, and only your own TREK server can
  ever ask.
- **Photos and the camera work.** Buttons that pick a photo, upload several at once, or take a
  picture now open the picker or the camera instead of doing nothing.
- **Downloads work.** Exports TREK offers on a phone — your MFA backup codes, a trip's calendar or
  GPX file — are saved to your Downloads folder instead of disappearing.
- Updated the TREK project links after the upstream repository was renamed.

## 1.3.1
- Updated the Android build tools and libraries under the hood. No changes to how the app looks or works.

## 1.3.0
- **Smooth dashboard scrolling.** Fixed pull-to-refresh getting in the way when you scroll
  the dashboard — it now only triggers when you're already at the top, so scrolling stays
  smooth.
- Updated to the latest Android libraries under the hood.

## 1.2.1
- **You stay signed in.** Fixed an issue where Embara could sign you out and make
  you log in again almost every time you reopened it. Your session now sticks
  reliably — even when your phone closes the app in the background.

## 1.2.0
- Refreshed app icon and branding.
- Fixed a layout glitch where a panel could get cut off on landscape tablets.
- Smaller app download.

## 1.1.1
- Fixed pull-to-refresh not working on some screens.

## 1.1.0
- Cleaner navigation — a native bar replaces the old pop-up overlay.
- Pull-to-refresh improvements.

## 1.0.2
- Security and privacy hardening for safer connections.

## 1.0.0
- First release of Embara.
